Questions

I've no experience. Is that okay?

Yes. Every session starts from zero, with all tools and PPE supplied.

Is this a qualification?

No. It's a taster to help you decide. We'll show you where qualifications fit.

Is it safe?

Every session opens with a safety brief, practice work is low-risk, and groups are small. No gas work and no live mains work by learners.

What do I do after?

You'll get a next-steps sheet. The report describes apprenticeship routes as roughly 4–5 years for electrical and plumbing and 3–4 years for carpentry [report estimate, verify before publishing].

Is it worth the money?

Sessions are €35–€50 each. You get a tradesperson's time, materials and something to take away, and you find out whether to go further.

Is it for women, over-40s, school-leavers?

It's for everyone. Rooms are mixed, and nobody is expected to know anything or fit a type.
